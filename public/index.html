RIGOROUS COMPLIANCE STACK

How EvidenceDesk Works

A multi-stage deterministic pipeline built specifically for SOC 2, ISO 27001, and CAIQ audit scrutiny.

01

Hybrid Retrieval & RRF Fusion

Combines exact lexical tokens (BM25Okapi) with 384-dimensional dense semantic vectors in Qdrant, fused deterministically using Reciprocal Rank Fusion (k=60).

02

Cross-Encoder Deep Reranking

The top-10 fused candidates are scored by BAAI/bge-reranker-large, evaluating query-passage token interactions to filter semantic noise.

03

Structured SLM Classifier

Local SLM (qwen2.5:3b) functions strictly as a classification engine with zero creative license, verified by an 8-stage Pydantic guard.

04

Adjacent Chunk Expansion

Automatically expands seed chunks with contiguous document context, preventing questions and answers separated across paragraph breaks from being missed.

05

Human Review Governance

Compliance officers inspect highlighted quotes, resolve conflicts, edit responses if necessary, and execute formal APPROVE or REJECT governance decisions.

06

SHA-256 Tamper-Evident Receipts

Every human review sign-off is logged in a cryptographically chained SQLite database (audit_trail.db) with instant tamper detection.
